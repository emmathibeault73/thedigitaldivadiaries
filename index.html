About your digital diva

Hi divas, I'm Emma.

I was born and raised on Long Island. I spent my summers out east in the Hamptons and Montauk, and I was always hopping on the LIRR into the city. Then I headed south to Florida Atlantic University in Boca Raton.

I've loved content for as long as I can remember. I've had Instagram since basically the day it came out, and I've vlogged pretty much everything I do. I had a squishy review channel, a slime review channel, you name it.

The Digital Diva Diaries is where that lifelong love of content gets to help other people. I treat every brand's feed like a diary: honest, personal, a little dramatic, and posted consistently. You bring the product and the personality, and I bring the plan, the content and the follow-through.

When I'm not creating, you'll find me at reformer or hot mat Pilates with a blueberry matcha or a honey sea salt iced latte in hand (always oat milk). My style is simple: not a lot of color, just espresso brown and slay.
